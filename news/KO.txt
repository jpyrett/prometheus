headline: Here Are 3 Consumer Stocks I'd Buy With $1,500 and Hold Through the Next Recession
<<<SUMMARY>>>
The article recommends three consumer stocks suitable for holding through an economic downturn: Royal Caribbean Cruises, Coca-Cola, and Home Depot. While these companies may experience short-term demand softfalls during a recession, their strong market positions, consistent performance, and reliable dividend histories suggest they will recover and provide long-term gains.
<<<INSIGHTS>>>
RCL | positive | Strong market share, high occupancy rates (112% load factor), 9% YoY revenue growth, and robust bookings for 2027 with high-teen percentage growth expected. Despite potential recession impacts on discretionary spending, the company's popularity suggests demand will bounce back.
KO | positive | Consumer staples with resilient demand, 7% YoY adjusted revenue growth driven by volume increases, and a proven track record of recovery from disruptions. Dividend King status with 64 consecutive years of dividend increases provides income stability during downturns.
HD | positive | Market leader in home improvement retail with positive same-store sales growth (2.2% in Q3) despite economic headwinds. Consistent dividend increases since 2010 and maintained dividends even during the Great Recession demonstrate financial resilience.
