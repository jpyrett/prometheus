Fable's skeleton is the right one: code scores, checked quotes, the anti-chase momentum rule, capital return from the filings you already parse, the underreaction test, gates, and the outcome ledger. The changes below are what keep that design from mixing unlike trades.

Scoring fixes

Rank every family on a 20-day excess over SPY after spread and slippage. A 5-day bounce and a 12-month buyback are not the same unit, and 12-minus-1 momentum plus the 52-week high plus residual momentum get one vote, not three.
Split completeness, agreement, and sample size instead of one confidence number. A single syndicated headline cannot read as certainty.
Use absolute thresholds when the candidate list is small. "Top quintile of five names" is not a quintile.
Turn value into a quality multiplier on the other families, not a standalone strategy.
For a $1,000 account, return one to three names and say they are one bet. A 35% sector cap describes a book this account cannot hold.
Behavior the first spec skipped

Every run reviews holdings and emits hold, add, reduce, or avoid. A broken thesis becomes the name RaiseFunds should sell. A cooldown stops the desk from re-buying yesterday's headline.
The Critic gets the rejects and the runner-up, with the gate that failed.
Regime uses breadth (share of names above their own 200-day average), not only SPY's average.
Size off the last few earnings-day moves when the next report falls inside the horizon. An ATR stop does not contain a gap.
Each family has an order style. Slow edges are limit orders.
New signals, mostly from data you already store

Residual return versus the sector ETF, so a reversal has to be the stock falling while its sector did not.
Accruals, receivables versus sales, stock-based pay, and dividend coverage from the 10-Q statements already parsed. These veto a "quality dip." They are not buy signals.
Year-over-year change in the 10-K risk factors, with a quote that must be new text.
Customers and suppliers named in the 10-K as the second-order names in a theme, after the headline names have already moved.
A Case 3 market brief: sector leadership, unusual volume, fresh events. "What's happening?" should lead with the tape.
Later, and only with a yes on new endpoints: insider purchase clusters, EPS revisions, short interest as a risk flag.
Left out on purpose: 13F cloning, social mentions as a buy signal, RSI by itself, calendar effects, and a separate weight for every event class.
