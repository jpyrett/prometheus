# A price floor, waiting limit orders, a typed share count with a recommendation (spec)

Jacob, 2026-11-21: "Build this: a floor price ("over $20 a share"); an order that waits in the market until the price drops to your limit; a typed share count combined with a recommendation ("buy 10 of MU if the recommendation passes" drops the 10, with a note)." His answers: **a watcher in the web server checks the waiting orders; a waiting order is a DAY order (it expires at that day's close); while it waits its cash (a purchase) or its shares (a sale) are SET ASIDE, for buys and sells.**

Design choices entry 140 (written by the main session). Builds on entry 138 (`design/request_numbers_spec.md`): the numbers are decided by code from how they were written; the model only has to agree.

## Part A. A price floor (`min_price`)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| F1 | A FLOOR is a `$` number (entry 138, D1) tied to one share AND written as a lower bound: `over`, `above`, `more than`, `at least`, `priced over`, `no less than` before it, or `or more` / `or higher` / `or better` after it ("over $20 a share", "stocks above $20", "sell 10 MSFT at $600 or more"). `TradeIntent.min_price`, `DirectOrder.min_price`, `WorkOrder.min_price` (`money.Price`, Decimal). Its span is never money. A ceiling written `or less` / `or lower` ("buy 10 of MU at $90 or less") is a `max_price` (add those two words to entry 138's ceiling words). The note "a minimum price is not supported" goes away. A floor above the ceiling raises `NeedsClarification`. |
| F2 | **Recommendation path:** the floor is a filter exactly like the ceiling: `recommend.within_price_limit` drops a candidate priced under it (one function for both bounds), `decisions.decide_trades` gives no decision to a purchase priced under it (note `UNDER_FLOOR`, told), the handshake refuses a purchase filled under it (`not_permitted`). |
| F3 | **Direct BUY with a floor** ("buy MU if it is over $20 a share"): placed only while the quote is at or above it; otherwise not placed and told (a buy that waits for the price to RISE is a stop order: not built). A direct SALE with a floor is a waiting sell limit (Part B). |
| F4 | Tests on the real model's replies (extend `scripts/capture_request_numbers_forms.py` and its fixture with: "Buy the top 3 AI stocks over $20 a share", "Recommend 3 oil stocks between $20 and $60 a share", "Sell 10 shares of MSFT at $600 or more", "Buy 10 of MU at $90 or less", "buy MSFT over $20 a share"; a few cents, allowed). |

## Part B. A waiting limit order (a DAY order, paper broker only)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| W1 | **Which orders wait.** Only a DIRECT order (the user's own, entry 87): a BUY with a ceiling (`max_price`) whose quote is ABOVE it, and a SALE with a floor (`min_price`) whose quote is BELOW it. Instead of "not placed" (entry 138 L5) it becomes an OPEN ORDER. On the recommendation path a limit stays a filter (F2, entry 138 L3/L4): nothing from a recommendation waits. A direct order whose quote already meets its limit fills at once, as today (at the quote, which is inside the limit). |
| W2 | **A day order.** It expires at the close of the trading session it was placed in (16:00 Eastern, 13:00 on an early-close day: `market_calendar`). Placed when the market is closed, it is for the NEXT trading session and expires at that session's close; the user is told the exact time ("waits until 4:00 pm ET Monday 2026-11-23"). Never carried to another day. |
| W3 | **Set aside (Jacob).** A waiting BUY reserves its most possible cost: `shares x limit` (shares typed), or `dollars` (an order in dollars, M2), to the cent (`trade_amount`). A waiting SALE reserves its shares. Reserved money and shares are NOT AVAILABLE to anything else: ONE door, `AccountStore.available_cash(account_id)` = cash - the reservations of the account's open orders, and `available_shares(account_id, symbol)`, and EVERY reader that decides what may be spent or sold reads it (the pot `sizing.pot_of`, `approve_trade` / `handshake.decide_approval`'s cash, RaiseFunds' holdings, `remove_funds`, direct-order sizing, `show_portfolio` shows cash, reserved and available). Grep for every reader of the account's cash and holdings; a test reads the source for any reader that bypasses the door (like the no-float test). Overspending is impossible: a placement whose reservation exceeds what is available is refused at placement. |
| W4 | **The store.** New table `trading.open_orders` in `sql/prometheus_accounts.sql` (additive, `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`): id, account_id, request_id, symbol, company, side, shares (`numeric(18,6)`, null for a dollars order), dollars (`numeric`, null for a shares order), limit_price, reserved_cash, reserved_shares, placed_at, expires_at, status (`open`, `filled`, `expired`, `cancelled`, `rejected`), closed_at, reason, trade_id (the booked trade). Placing and reserving is ONE transaction with the row-level locks entry 82 uses (`FOR UPDATE` on the account and the position): placing cannot race a fill or another placement. Filling books the trade, marks the order `filled` and releases the reservation in ONE transaction (`book_trade` gains the order id; a fill can never book twice: the status is checked under the lock). Expiring and cancelling release it in one transaction. Money and shares are Decimal through the doors (entry 112). |
| W5 | **The watcher.** In the web server (`prometheus/application/web/`), started with the app and stopped with it: every `orders.watch_seconds` (config, 60) DURING the regular session only, it quotes the symbols of the open orders (ONE `request_quotes` through the Data Clerk for all of them; Finnhub, in the licence) and for each order whose quote meets its limit (buy: quote <= limit; sale: quote >= limit) FILLS it through the EXISTING funnel: a one-ticket work order run by the Trader with no model, the paper venue (fill at the quote, which is inside the limit), `approve_trade` (which reads the order's reservation as available to THIS order only), `verify_fill`, `record_fill`/`book_trade`. No second booking path. A buy in dollars gets `shares.buys(min(dollars, reserved), fill price)` (fractions only when the book holds them, M3; whole shares otherwise). At and after the close it EXPIRES every order whose `expires_at` has passed (also at server start: nothing stale survives a restart). One watcher per database: a Postgres advisory lock, so a second server does not double-check. A failed quote is retried at the next tick (an outage never fills, never expires early). Every fill, expiry and cancel is told on the page as an event and written to the audit log line of the trade. |
| W6 | **The user.** The run's last message says it: "Waiting: buy 10 MU at $90.00 or less (now $95.12), $900.00 set aside, until 4:00 pm ET today." The page's account panel lists the open orders with a CANCEL button (`DELETE /api/orders/{id}`, releases the reservation); `GET /api/accounts/{id}/orders`. A command `python -m prometheus.agents.account_manager.orders --account N [--list | --cancel ID | --check]` (`--list` and `--cancel` are free; `--check` quotes, Finnhub). The session CLI says when no server is running that the order is watched only while the page's server runs. |
| W7 | **Nothing else changes.** No size limit, no risk rule (AGENTS.md). Never a live broker. The Time Machine makes no direct orders, so nothing changes there. Entry 82 holds: a fill that fails is `rejected`, shown, never retried, and its reservation is released. |
| W8 | Config: `configs/account_manager_v1.json` section `orders`: `{"watch_seconds": 60}`; validated like the other sections. |

## Part C. A typed share count with a recommendation

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| C1 | "Buy 10 of MU if the recommendation passes", "buy 10 of MU and 5 of AMD if they're recommended": `TradeIntent.shares_of: dict[symbol or company, Shares]`, read from the words by the same code as a direct order's shares (entry 138 D2). "Buy 5 shares of each of the top 3 AI stocks" / "5 shares each": `TradeIntent.shares_each: Shares` (`EACH_CUE`, entry 125). |
| C2 | The request stays on the recommendation path (the words asked for a recommendation). An APPROVED (or neutral, entry 91) purchase of a stock with typed shares gets those shares as its sizing `wanted` limit, binding `asked` (the same mechanism as the how-much answer, entry 85); `shares_each` applies to every approved purchase. The funds are still the only check: with too little cash the sizing gives fewer and says so (round robin, entry 106). A DENIED one buys nothing (unchanged). |
| C3 | `said_how_much()` is true when every stock named has typed shares (or `shares_each` is set): Ask mode does not ask how much again; it still asks to approve each trade (entry 79). The note "the 10 shares are not used" goes away. |
| C4 | Tests: the real captured replies already in `request_numbers_forms.json` ("Buy 10 of MU if the recommendation passes") plus "buy 5 shares of each of the top 3 oil stocks if recommended" (capture, cents); `decide_trades` on a real review capture with `wanted` from C1. |
