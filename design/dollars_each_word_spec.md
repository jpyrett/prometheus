# "$3,000 each": one amount written once is the limit of EVERY stock it names (entry 125)

Jacob, 2026-11-20: *add support for "each" ... each means every individual stock.* Entry 122 (spec `design/direct_dollars_each_spec.md`) made `DirectOrder.dollars` each stock's limit, and its count rule `intent.check_dollars_each` refuses an amount written once from being the dollars of two orders (the model had read "$5,000 of MU and AMD" as $5,000 each, which would spend $10,000 on a request for $5,000).
