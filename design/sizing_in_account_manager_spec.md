# Sizing moves to the Account Manager (design choices entry 80)

Decided by Jacob on 2026-11-14, in his words: *"The Advisor only generates recommendations. The Account Manager agent can use ReAct to clarify with the user, in Ask mode, how much to buy."* and *"The Advisor and Critic only deal with Recommendations."*

## The principle

| agent | what it decides | what it does NOT decide |
|---|---|---|
| Advisor | which companies to recommend, their stance and score, the evidence, the thesis, and the PLAN of the trade: entry zone, stop, target, time stop, order style, invalidation (prices and dates) | **how much to buy or sell** |
| Critic | whether each recommendation is approved or denied: accuracy, evidence, risk fit of the COMPANY against the user's risk tolerance (age and horizon included), timing, value, chart | **how much to buy or sell**, and nothing about the user's money |
| Account Manager | **how much to buy or sell**: from the user's money, the user's limits and what the user said; in Ask mode it can clarify with the user (ReAct); and whether a trade is permitted (the existing `decide_trades`) | which companies, or whether a company is a good idea |
| Trader | executes what the Account Manager decided (unchanged) | anything else |

## The Account Manager's sizing (new, code only)

`prometheus/agents/account_manager/sizing.py` and `configs/account_manager_v1.json` (every threshold in the file, never in the code, loaded and checked as the other configs: `extra="forbid"`).

`size_purchases(...)` works out, for the recommendations the Critic approved to BUY (an `open` or an `add`), how many shares, in this order:

1. **Equity**: the cash plus the holdings at their current prices (`AccountManager.request_prices`, one Data Clerk quote order for the symbols held; a price that cannot be had falls back to the cost basis and says so).
2. **Caps per purchase** (the arithmetic that was `critic/risk.size_caps` and the Advisor's, moved): position (`max_position_pct x equity - held value`, whole shares), loss (`max_loss_per_trade_pct x equity / stop distance`, from the plan's stop), order (`max_order_dollars / price`), liquidity (`max_adv_pct x average daily dollars / price`, `max_adv_pct` in `configs/account_manager_v1.json`, the value the Advisor used), money (the cash). The price is the Critic's deal price (`review.timing.deal_price`), else the plan's entry mid. A cap that cannot be worked out is `None` and is said; a purchase whose caps allow no whole share is not made (`no_funds` or a new code `no_room` with the reason).
3. **Exposure** across the purchases (`max_new_exposure_pct x equity`, in the order of the recommendations' rank, the running total as `critic/risk.exposure_cap` did).
4. **What the user asked for**: by default each purchase is sized to the most the caps allow ("as much as I can"); `allocation="even"` then gives equal dollars (the existing `decisions.balance_evenly`, now over the caps instead of over the Critic's approvals); a `budget` caps the total.
5. Each decision records how it was sized: `decision["size"] = {"shares", "caps": {...}, "binding": "position"|"loss"|"order"|"liquidity"|"exposure"|"money"|"budget"|"even_split"|None, "equity": ...}`.

`decisions.decide_trades` keeps its permission rules (a denial is final, an expired approval, Ask or Auto and the mark, requires_human) and sizes the purchases through `sizing.size_purchases` where it used `review["approved_shares"]`. A SALE (a `reduce`): the whole holding unless the user said otherwise (the Account Manager's, as before: it is never automatic in Auto mode). The funds check that was `trade_plan.funds_check` (can the money be raised from holdings in profit) moves here: `funds_feasible` and the note go on the decision.

## Ask mode: the Account Manager clarifies with ReAct (phase 2)

When the mode is Ask (the human-in-the-loop mode), the Critic approved at least one purchase, and the user has not already said how much (no amount in the request: no percentage, no dollars, no share count, no "as much as I can" or "all of it"), the Account Manager clarifies with the user before it sends anything to the Trader. It runs a bounded ReAct loop with its model (`account_manager` role): THOUGHT, then an ACTION that is one of the tools `ask_user(question)` or `set_sizing(unit, amount, scope, allocation)`, then the OBSERVATION (the user's answer, as data). At most three questions.

**What the user may answer** (Jacob, 2026-11-14: "what percentage, dollar amount to buy or number of shares"): a **percentage** of the account ("20% of my account"), a **dollar amount** ("$3,000"), a **number of shares** ("10 shares"), or "as much as I can". `scope` is `each` (per company) or `total` (split over the approved companies; `allocation` `even` or `as_much_as_possible`). The model only phrases the questions and reads the answer into that structure. CODE does the arithmetic: percentage x equity / price, dollars / price, or the shares as given, all in whole shares, then the caps of the profile (position, loss, order, liquidity, money, exposure). A request above a cap is cut to the cap and says which cap; when the cap is the user's own limit, the Account Manager may ask once whether to raise it ("your limit is 10% of your account in one stock, about $1,500: raise it for this purchase?") and a limit raised by the user's answer is for that purchase only. A number the user did not type (or that is above the cash, or a percentage outside 0 to 100) is refused by the validator, and the loop asks again or falls back to the default sizing, saying so.

**The user then approves the proposed order**: the loop ends by showing what will be bought (symbol, shares, price, cost, the binding cap, the cash left) and asking Y/N. That approval is the human-in-the-loop approval of the trade (`will_trade` `yes_user`, as `decide_trades` already records an approval of a pending trade); only then does the work order go on to the Trader. A "no" leaves the trade `pending_user` and nothing is sent.

In Auto mode it never asks: it sizes by the rules above (as much as the caps allow, in rank order, or the even split or amount the user's request already gave). The facts the model is shown (cash, the approved purchases with their prices, the limits, what was already said) are numbers made by code; no news text and no thesis reach it. A model that fails or answers nonsense leaves the default sizing and says so.

## Amendments of 2026-11-14 (after the Ask-mode approval fix, design choices entry 79: the sizing move is entry 80)

1. **No cash is reserved** (Jacob: "do not reserve any cash. Any available funds in the portfolio can be used"). The `money` cap of EVERY purchase in `sizing.py` is all the available cash, whatever the other purchases cost: no running cash. The profile's exposure cap and the even split / budget stay (a limit, and equal dollars with a total of at most the cash or the budget). The Trader checks the cash again when it places each order (it fills what the cash pays for, or rejects `insufficient_cash`).
2. **`decide_trades` already has the order the sizing must keep** (entry 79): (a) a row per review (denied, expired, no funds, `yes_auto`, or `pending_user` for an Ask or escalated row); (b) the even split over the purchases that trade AND those that wait for the user (`balance_evenly(..., pending=True)`); (c) `_ask_the_user`, one at a time, each card (`_card`) showing the FINAL shares and cost ("WOULD BE PLACED") and the whole proposal, which `terminal_approver` prints once first. A "no" re-splits nothing. In Step 2, (b) becomes `sizing.size_purchases` (the card's shares and cost come from the sized decision, the `approved_shares` of the Critic's review go away); in Phase 2 the clarifying questions come BEFORE (b) and the per-trade Y/N of (c) stays as the human approval before the Trader.
3. `session.py` now has `approver_for(parts_approver, ask, out)` and `decision_lines(order, mode)`, and `test_prometheus.sh` defaults the mode to Ask. Step 2 changes `use_of_money_lines` (it reads the Critic's caps) and nothing else of these.
4. `_funds` in `decisions.py` still reads the Advisor's `trade.funds_feasible` flag (true in every real capture, because the Advisor checked its own size against the whole cash). Step 2 replaces it with the Account Manager's own funds check (`sizing.funds_check`) on the sized purchase.

5. **Auto mode splits the funds ROUND-ROBIN** (Jacob: "In Auto mode, the strategy is to take the available funds and use round-robin. Buy a share of first stock, buy a share of the next stock, and keep buying shares until all the desired (requested) funds to use are used up."). `sizing.py` gets the allocation `round_robin` (ALLOCATIONS = "even", "round_robin", "as_much_as_possible"): the pot is the funds requested (the user's `budget`, else the cash, never more than the cash); each round buys ONE share of each purchase still open, in rank order, while the purchase is under its own limits (position, loss, order, liquidity), the exposure room has room for another share and the pot pays for it; a purchase that cannot take another share closes and remembers why; it stops when none is open. It is arithmetic in code: each stock ends with a FINAL share count and the Trader places ONE trade per stock, never one per share. `decide_trades` uses it when the effective mode is Auto and the user asked for no split; "split evenly" in the user's words still gives equal DOLLARS (`even`); Ask mode with no split asked keeps `as_much_as_possible`, and the user can choose any of the three when the Account Manager asks (Phase 2). The profile's limits still apply: when they stop the round-robin before the pot is used, the report says which limit did.


## Phase 2 built (2026-11-15, design choices entry 85)

Built as specified, with these differences, all decided in the build: (1) the question is asked only when somebody is at the keyboard and the request did not say how much (`TradeIntent.said_how_much`, a check by code); (2) `scope` and `allocation` are checked against the user's words as well as the amount (a split the user did not ask for is refused: a real run showed the model choosing "even" on its own); `round_robin` is allowed as an allocation (it was not in the spec's list); (3) a number of shares in total is shared out over the companies in rank order, and what a limit cuts is not moved; (4) the "raise the limit?" question is offered for the position and exposure limits only, worded by code with no model, once; (5) the amount asked for is a limit of the purchase in `sizing.size_purchases` (`wanted`, `Sized.asked`, binding `asked`), not one of the seven caps; (6) `AccountManager.decide_trades(..., clarify=, said_how_much=, request_text=)` and `desk.build_desk(..., clarify=)` carry the channel, and `session.clarifier_for(ask, out)` makes the terminal's. The thresholds (three questions, two refusals) are in `configs/account_manager_v1.json` section `clarify`.
