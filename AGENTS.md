## Where things are

- `prometheus/langgraph/desk.py` is the top-level LangGraph: `account_manager -> data_clerk -> advisor`, one work order as its state (`--critic` adds the Critic and the decisions, `--raise-funds` the funds node, `--trade` the Trader after them).
- `prometheus/agents/data_clerk/data_clerk_agent.py` is the Data Clerk agent (`DataClerk`: LLM, tools, a loop, a budget, a citation check). Its call for the other agents is `request_data(work_order)`.
- `prometheus/agents/data_clerk/data_toolbox.py` is the toolbox it uses (`DataToolbox`): plain Python, no LLM. It is not an agent.
- `prometheus/agents/account_manager/account_manager.py` is the Account Manager agent (`AccountManager`, an LLM like the Advisor): `raise_funds` is the Tree of Thought for what to sell to buy a stock; the tree is `prometheus/reasoning/raise_funds.py`; the sample portfolio is `configs/portfolios/jacob_pyrett.json`; the record of how it was built is `design/week4_tot_guide.md`. Its prices come from `DataClerk.request_quotes`. The account book (entry 72) is the same class with a Postgres store: `add_funds`, `list_portfolio`, `set_risk_level`, the gain and the counts, and `users` for the name and account number. It is also the ONLY owner of the funds while the Trader trades (entry 81): `trading_portfolio(portfolio, book)` opens a run and holds the working portfolio, `approve_trade`, `preview_trade`, `record_fill`, `apply_earlier_fills` and `current_portfolio` are the Trader's doors to it, and the book's own `book_trade` transaction is the guard that cannot be raced (entry 82: it takes row-level `FOR UPDATE` locks on the account row and on EVERY position row, re-reads the account before it commits and rolls back on a difference; `record_fill` SELECTs the account before it and again after the commit and checks that the trade row is there and the totals are different). The user talks to the Account Manager only: it has a `screen` (the session sets it to its own `out`), `tell_user` and `show_portfolio`, and when a trade fails it shows the error and the account as it stands at once. Schema `sql/prometheus_accounts.sql`, container `scripts/setup_postgres.sh`, passwords in `secrets/postgres_local.txt` (gitignored). `--account` prints one account and does not call a quote or a model. HOW MUCH to buy or sell is its alone (entries 80 and 91), and the ONLY check is that the funds cover the order: `prometheus/agents/account_manager/sizing.py` (`size_purchases`: the cash, a budget, the shares asked for, the allocation `round_robin` (THE DEFAULT IN BOTH MODES, entry 106: one share of each purchase in turn, never everything into the first) or `even` (the user said "split it evenly", the ONLY thing that chooses it, a run of neutral companies alone included, entry 109: equal dollars as close as whole shares allow, and what the equal amounts leave over is spent one share at a time to the purchase with the smallest amount, entry 108; no fractional shares yet) that only divides the user's funds; there is no `as_much_as_possible` any more), called by `decisions.decide_trades`. THE ACCOUNT MANAGER DOES NO RISK MANAGEMENT (Jacob, entry 91): there is NO profile size limit (position, loss, order, exposure, liquidity; the profile has no such field and a test reads the source), do not add one. In Ask mode, when the request did not say how much (`TradeIntent.said_how_much()`) and somebody is at the keyboard, the Account Manager first ASKS how much with a bounded ReAct loop (`agents/account_manager/clarify.py`, `AccountManager.clarify_sizing`, entry 85, thresholds in `configs/account_manager_v1.json` section `clarify`): at most three questions; the model only words them and reads the answer, CODE checks the answer against what the user typed and does the arithmetic, the shares asked for are the sizing's `wanted` limit (binding `asked`). AUTO MODE NEVER ASKS (Jacob, entry 104): it trades every recommendation the Critic APPROVED (`yes_auto`) and there is NO second mark (no `auto_min_score` anywhere: the profile, the Critic's config, the session flag and the page's Strict are without one; no `escalated` on a decision); a DENIED recommendation is never traded in either mode and the Account Manager returns it to the user with the Critic's reason (`decisions.denied_lines`, printed by `session.decision_lines`). The Account Manager is the ONLY agent that touches the holdings (entry 105): a sale comes only from the user's own direct order (`origin: "direct"`) or from RaiseFunds, never from a recommendation (`work_order.verify_trade` and `verify_trades` refuse it); `reduce` and `add` are only its own decisions for the user's own orders (`direct.py`). A neutral candidate carries an `exit_plan` (entry 134: a stop 2 x ATR under the close and a 20-session time stop, `neutral` in `configs/advisor_v1.json`, no target): it is read ONLY by the Proving Ground and the Live view, the Trader's tickets do not change and nothing is sold in a live account by it. A NEUTRAL recommendation counts as approved (entry 91): with `ALLOW_NEUTRAL_RECOMMENDATIONS=true` (read by the Account Manager through `rag.config.setting`, never by reading `.env`; unset means false) EVERY company the Advisor scored neutral, whoever named it and whether or not the user already holds some (entries 105 and 107), with no failed gate, counts as an approval and gets a trade decision with `origin: "neutral"` (`agents/account_manager/neutral.py`; Auto `yes_auto`, Ask asks the user). The number the request asked for is kept (`AccountManager.decide_trades(..., count)`, the form's own `count`, never the default of 3): the Critic's approved recommendations first, then the neutral ones by the Advisor's score, a denied recommendation does not count, and a neutral company beyond the number gets NO decision (the user is told). Approved and neutral purchases are sized TOGETHER in one `sizing.size_purchases` call (in `decisions.decide_trades`), and `work_order.verify_trade` accepts it only while the Advisor's own neutral record is on `order.candidates` with no Critic review beside it. A Critic denial is final and a bearish stance or a failed gate trades nothing. In Ask mode, when the request has no amount ("I want to buy MU and AMD"), the Account Manager asks for the number first with the same questions as for any purchase (entry 92; one set covers the Critic's purchases and the neutral ones). Tests `tests/test_neutral_recommendation.py` (real capture `tests/fixtures/session_real/pam_smith_neutral.json`).

- `prometheus/agents/critic/` is the Critic (`Critic.review_recommendations(order, risk, now)`: it never receives the portfolio and it does not size, `RiskView` holds no size limit, and it judges recommendations to BUY and nothing about the holdings (entry 105: a record that is not a purchase is a `record_defect`; no module reads `held_shares` or `cost_basis`); nine steps in `review.py`; every threshold is in `configs/critic_v1.json`; the spec is `design/critic_v1_final_spec.md`, the build notes `design/how_to_code_critic.md`, the reasons entries 56 to 59). Every finding is scored against the outcomes by `prometheus/proving/report_card.py` (`./test_proving.sh critic-report`, the Track Record tab): a new denial rule needs a reason in words AND outcomes on data it was not found on, and thresholds are never tuned on the outcomes. **CRITIC v3 (entry 137, spec `design/critic_v3_two_reasons_spec.md`, config version 3): A DENIAL NEEDS TWO REASONS.** `critic/reasons.py` is the closed class table (a test fails for a blocker code in no class or two): a FAULT (record_defect, record_not_reviewable, symbol_unknown, price_mismatch, source_missing, source_altered, `data_unreliable`, market_closed, quote_stale, no_live_price, bars_stale, record_expired, time_stop_passed, stop_already_hit, and anything that asks for a person) and a PROFILE finding (risk_above_profile, symbol_excluded, sector_excluded, price_below_profile_min) deny ALONE; every other denying finding is a SIGN of the group `news` (fresh_negative_event, fresh_negative_news, event_not_supported, analysts_bearish), `performance` (trend_not_confirmed, dead_cat_bounce, falling_knife, price_below_zone, chart_bearish, poor_performance) or `price` (overpriced, valuation_extreme, price_left_zone); ONE bad group approves ("one reason against it"), TWO deny. A new reason must name its class. `data_mismatch`/`data_unverified` of ONE metric are warnings (no nitpicking); THE SCORE NO LONGER DENIES (`approve_min_score` is gone) and gets a `pattern_bonus` (net chart points x 0.25, max 8). Eight more patterns in `critic/patterns.py` from the Data Clerk's metric `close_history` (split-adjusted to the last day; the Critic never calls `request_bars`); a neckline pattern counts only when broken within `chart.pattern_recent_sessions`. The report card carries `groups` and `as_v3`. THE VERDICT IS APPROVED OR DENIED, NOTHING BETWEEN (entry 103): a finding that asks for a person to look denies the review (`needs_human_review`; the score is not capped). It imports nothing of the Advisor's scoring code (a test enforces it). - A RUN NEVER SPENDS MORE THAN ITS MONEY (entry 111, the spec is `design/dollar_orders_spec.md`; Jacob: *"Slippage is not allowed. This is real money. It is impossible to spend more than the amount in the portfolio and budget. ... It is OK to have unused money left over but never is allowed to spend more than is available."*). Five rules. (M4) `costs.slippage_bps` is 0 in `configs/trader_v1.json`: a paper fill is at the quote, a backtest fill at the open (the simulator keeps its argument; recordings made with 5 bps are history). (M1) THE POT of a run is the budget the user gave, else the cash, never more than the cash (`sizing.pot_of`): every sized decision carries it (`decision["size"]["pot"]`, `work_order.decision_pot`), the handshake never approves a purchase past what is left of it (`AccountManager.spent_of_pot`: from the filled purchases on `order.trades` AND the fills told in this run, never from memory alone; `handshake.decide_approval(..., spendable=)`; a purchase with no pot, a direct order with typed shares or a RaiseFunds purchase, is held by the cash only), and `work_order.verify_trades` fails a work order whose purchases cost more than the pot or carry two. (M2) AN ORDER IN DOLLARS: a decision may carry `dollars` (the most it may cost, to the cent; `work_order.decision_dollars`); `shares`, `est_cost` and `price` are then the ESTIMATE at the sizing price, `Trade.dollars` is copied by `tickets.py`, the Account Manager turns the dollars into shares AT THE FILL PRICE (`decide_approval(..., dollars=)` = `shares.buys(min(dollars, pot left, cash), price)`, read from the decision, never from the Trader's record: fewer shares than the estimate when the price is higher, more when lower), the broker fills the shares approved, and `verify_fill` checks the fill's AMOUNT against the dollars. Orders in dollars are: every purchase of a fractional even split (except one held to the shares the user asked for) and every purchase of a direct order sized from money; a round-robin purchase of the recommendation path, an answer in shares, typed shares, a sale and a funding sale are orders in shares. With fractions off nothing is an order in dollars. (M3) A DIRECT ORDER WITH MONEY ("Buy $5,000 of MSFT", a stock with no number: `AccountManager._size_direct`, `size_purchases(..., fractional_leftover=True)`) buys as many whole shares as the money pays for (round robin, as ever) and a FRACTION of a share with what is left, on the stock with the fewest shares; dollars or a percentage for EACH stock is `shares.buys(the amount, its price)` (`wanted` may be a quantity); the recommendation path's round robin stays whole shares and leaves its leftover as cash. (M5) A SHARE COUNT IS A NUMBER: a bool or a string, "2" too, is an error at every door (`shares.quantity`, `raise_funds._clean`, `SizingChoice` in shares, `_check_purchase`); money from a count and a price is `trade_amount` (decimal, half up to the cent), never a float product rounded. No size limit, risk rule or minimum order was added. A DOLLAR AMOUNT FOR EACH STOCK is that stock's limit (entries 122 and 125): `DirectOrder.dollars` ("$3,000 of XOM and $3,000 of MSFT"), and `TradeIntent.each` ("$3,000 each", "apiece", checked against the closed word list `intent.EACH_CUE`; on the recommendation path every approved and neutral purchase is capped at the amount); an amount written once without such a word is ONE total (`budget`); a request that wrote an amount and whose reading has none is a request to clarify, never all the cash (`check_amount_was_read`). Tests: `test_pot.py`, `test_dollar_orders.py`, `test_direct_dollars.py`, `test_share_count_is_a_number.py`, `test_fractional_session.py` and the live session `tests/fixtures/session_real/auto_even_split_dollars.json`.
- MONEY, PRICES AND SHARE COUNTS ARE `decimal.Decimal` IN THE ACCOUNT'S CODE AND IN NOTHING ELSE (entry 112, the spec is `design/decimal_money_spec.md`; Jacob, 2026-11-16: *"Need to always use decimal.Decimal type when dealing with money and share counts."*). `prometheus/common/money.py` is the one home of the doors (`money`: to the cent, more than two decimals refused; `price`: four places, half up; `rate`; `digits`; `exact`), the pydantic types (`Money`, `PositiveMoney`, `NonNegativeMoney`, `Price`, `Rate`; `shares.Shares`) and the ONE JSON encoder (`json_number`, `jsonable`, `dumps`, `Jsonable`: a Decimal is a JSON NUMBER, a whole value an integer, never a string; it refuses a value with more digits than a float holds). A Decimal is made from DIGITS (`Decimal("19.99")`, `Decimal(3)`, or `Decimal(str(x))` inside a door), never `Decimal(19.99)`; a result is rounded once, on purpose, by name (`cents`, `price_scale`, `trade_amount`, `shares.buys`); a bool or a string ("2" too) is refused at every door; after the door there is no float: `Decimal * float` and `money.exact(float)` raise TypeError and that is WANTED, so never quiet one with `float(...)` or `Decimal(str(x))` where it is raised: find where the float came in and convert it at its DOOR (a quote: `AccountManager._prices_of`; a bar: `simulator.bar_price`; the Critic's deal price and the Advisor's plan: `decisions._price_of`, `tickets._price`). Tests: `test_decimal_money.py` (the doors and the encoder), `test_decimal_exactness.py` (the identities, on real data), `test_no_float_in_the_account.py`; a test whose expected value is money, a price or a share count compares with `Decimal("4907.92")`, never a float and never `pytest.approx`.
