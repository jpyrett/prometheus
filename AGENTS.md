## Where things are

- `prometheus/langgraph/desk.py` is the top-level LangGraph: `account_manager -> data_clerk -> advisor`, one work order as its state (`--critic` adds the Critic and the decisions, `--raise-funds` the funds node, `--trade` the Trader after them).
- `prometheus/agents/data_clerk/data_clerk_agent.py` is the Data Clerk agent (`DataClerk`: LLM, tools, a loop, a budget, a citation check). Its call for the other agents is `request_data(work_order)`.
- `prometheus/agents/data_clerk/data_toolbox.py` is the toolbox it uses (`DataToolbox`): plain Python, no LLM. It is not an agent.
- `prometheus/agents/account_manager/account_manager.py` is the Account Manager agent (`AccountManager`, an LLM like the Advisor): `raise_funds` is the Tree of Thought for what to sell to buy a stock; the tree is `prometheus/reasoning/raise_funds.py`; the sample portfolio is `configs/portfolios/jacob_pyrett.json`; the record of how it was built is `design/week4_tot_guide.md`. Its prices come from `DataClerk.request_quotes`. The account book (entry 72) is the same class with a Postgres store: `add_funds`, `list_portfolio`, `set_risk_level`, the gain and the counts, and `users` for the name and account number. It is also the ONLY owner of the funds while the Trader trades (entry 81): `trading_portfolio(portfolio, book)` opens a run and holds the working portfolio, `approve_trade`, `preview_trade`, `record_fill`, `apply_earlier_fills` and `current_portfolio` are the Trader's doors to it, and the book's own `book_trade` transaction is the guard that cannot be raced (entry 82: it takes row-level `FOR UPDATE` locks on the account row and on EVERY position row, re-reads the account before it commits and rolls back on a difference; `record_fill` SELECTs the account before it and again after the commit and checks that the trade row is there and the totals are different). The user talks to the Account Manager only: it has a `screen` (the session sets it to its own `out`), `tell_user` and `show_portfolio`, and when a trade fails it shows the error and the account as it stands at once. Schema `sql/prometheus_accounts.sql`, container `scripts/setup_postgres.sh`, passwords in `secrets/postgres_local.txt` (gitignored). `--account` prints one account and does not call a quote or a model. HOW MUCH to buy or sell is its alone (entries 80 and 91), and the ONLY check is that the funds cover the order: `prometheus/agents/account_manager/sizing.py` (`size_purchases`: the cash, a budget, the shares asked for, the allocation `round_robin` (Auto's default), `even` or `as_much_as_possible` that only divides the user's funds; no cash is reserved), called by `decisions.decide_trades`. THE ACCOUNT MANAGER DOES NO RISK MANAGEMENT (Jacob, entry 91): there is NO profile size limit (position, loss, order, exposure, liquidity; the profile has no such field and a test reads the source), do not add one. In Ask mode, when the request did not say how much (`TradeIntent.said_how_much()`) and somebody is at the keyboard, the Account Manager first ASKS how much with a bounded ReAct loop (`agents/account_manager/clarify.py`, `AccountManager.clarify_sizing`, entry 85, thresholds in `configs/account_manager_v1.json` section `clarify`): at most three questions; the model only words them and reads the answer, CODE checks the answer against what the user typed and does the arithmetic, the shares asked for are the sizing's `wanted` limit (binding `asked`). Auto mode never asks. A NEUTRAL recommendation counts as approved (entry 91): with `ALLOW_NEUTRAL_RECOMMENDATIONS=true` (read by the Account Manager through `rag.config.setting`, never by reading `.env`; unset means false) a company the user NAMED that the Advisor scored neutral, with no failed gate and not held, gets a trade decision with `origin: "neutral"` (`agents/account_manager/neutral.py`; equal dollars, no limit; Auto `yes_auto`, Ask asks the user), and `work_order.verify_trade` accepts it only while the Advisor's own neutral record is on `order.candidates` with no Critic review beside it. A Critic denial is final and a bearish stance or a failed gate trades nothing. In Ask mode, when the request has no amount ("I want to buy MU and AMD"), the Account Manager asks for the number first with the same questions as for any purchase (entry 92; one set covers the Critic's purchases and the neutral ones). Tests `tests/test_neutral_recommendation.py` (real capture `tests/fixtures/session_real/pam_smith_neutral.json`).
- `Advisor.generate_recommendations(order, portfolio, case, today)` in `prometheus/agents/advisor/advisor.py` is the Advisor's recommendation step (Advisor v1): the twelve steps are `advisor/recommend.py`, the rest of `advisor/` is its parts (`families`, `blend` (the 40/30/20/10 score: news, performance, historical, filings), `calls`, `gates`, `quality`, `ranking`, `trade_plan`, `events`, `thesis`, `record`), every threshold is in `configs/advisor_v1.json`, and the guide is `design/week5_advisor_implementation.md`. The trade plan is prices and dates (entry zone, stop, target, time stop, order style, invalidation) and has NO size: the Advisor recommends no amount, a `reduce` has none either, and it does not use the user's cash or equity (it reads the portfolio only for the holdings; a test reads its source, entry 80). The numbers come from `DataClerk.request_metrics` (`agents/data_clerk/metrics_catalog.py`, `technicals.py`, `fundamentals.py`, `bars.py`); the ledger is `prometheus/memory/ledger.py`. `advise` (v0) is untouched.
- `prometheus/agents/critic/` is the Critic (`Critic.review_recommendations(order, risk, now)`: it never receives the portfolio and it does not size, `RiskView` holds no size limit; nine steps in `review.py`; every threshold is in `configs/critic_v1.json`; the spec is `design/critic_v1_final_spec.md`, the build notes `design/how_to_code_critic.md`, the reasons entries 56 to 59). It asks the Data Clerk AGAIN (`request_metrics`, `request_quotes`, `request_verification`) and compares; its model (Z.ai GLM 5.3, `CRITIC_MODEL`) only labels quotes. It imports nothing of the Advisor's scoring code (a test enforces it). Its risk score has five measures and NO liquidity, stop-distance or market-cap measure (entries 92 and 97; there is no market capitalization and no net payout yield anywhere: the capital_return family passes on the share count), and the Advisor has NO liquidity gate or input (entries 93 and 94: four gates, `no_metrics`, `freshness`, `red_flag`, `event_risk`, which are also the only ones the Critic enforces, `critic/integrity.GATES`); do not add a liquidity rule anywhere. `prometheus/agents/account_manager/decisions.py` turns its verdicts into trade decisions (Ask or Auto); `prometheus/common/user_profile.py` is the profile, `market_calendar.py` the trading calendar.
- `./test_recommendations.sh` runs the whole thing from a typed request (`prometheus/application/recommendations.py`: symbols, a list, a company, a brand or a phrase, to a ranked report; Advisor on gpt-oss-120b): fetching is paid, ask first. It now also runs the Critic by default (Finnhub and the critic's model: `--no-critic` leaves it out).
- `prometheus/agents/trader/` is the Trader (`Trader.execute_trades(order, account, portfolio, now, funding, approver, book)`, the ten steps are `execute.py`; a model, gpt-oss-120b at temperature 0, with four tools that cannot name a symbol, a side or a number of shares, then a code sweep that places what it left; `tickets`, `broker`, `simulator`, `record`, `config` (every threshold is in `configs/trader_v1.json`), `report`, and `replay.py`, the replay command). It executes only what `decisions.py` permitted, against a SIMULATED broker: `paper` at the current quote (Finnhub) or `backtest` at the next open of the stored bars; never live. It reaches the world only through the Account Manager (`request_prices`, `request_bars`, `trading_portfolio`, `apply_earlier_fills`, `current_portfolio`, `approve_trade`, `preview_trade`, `record_fill`; a test reads its imports and fails on any other call) and it holds NO cash and NO portfolio of its own (entry 81, a test reads its source): the Trader and the Account Manager shake hands on EVERY trade, one at a time. For each trade the Account Manager reads the funds as they are now and approves the shares (`approve_trade`, `agents/account_manager/handshake.py`: `Approval`), the Trader fills at most those, checks that one fill (`work_order.verify_fill`) and reports it AT ONCE (`record_fill`: `Booking`, applied to the Account Manager's working portfolio or, with `book`, booked in ONE Postgres transaction), and only then asks for the next trade, so it cannot overdraw the funds; nothing is reserved. A trade that FAILS (the book refused it, it was rolled back, its booking could not be confirmed, its output check found something) is `rejected`, shown to the user by the Account Manager and NEVER retried, and the Trader places the next trade: nothing halts a run (entry 82; `PaperBroker.halted` is gone). The handshake never waits for a next trade: an approval is asked only for a trade about to be placed, a refused or unfilled trade has no `record_fill`, after the last answer the run goes straight to its end, and with no ticket there is no handshake at all (a test records the calls and reads the source for a wait). `notify_account_manager` (the model's tool) now only closes the run. In the desk it is the `trader` node (`--trade [paper|backtest]`, `--no-trader-model`; `--account` and `--book` use a saved account), in the runner `./test_recommendations.sh --trade`. The spec is `design/trader_v1_final_spec.md` (amended at its end for the handshake), the handshake's `design/trader_handshake_spec.md`, the build notes `design/how_to_code_trader.md`, the reasons entries 73 to 75 and 81.
- `./test_prometheus.sh --session` (`prometheus/application/session.py`, entry 78) is a user session end to end: it creates a user and an account in Postgres, takes a request in the user's own words (`AccountManager.understand_request`, `agents/account_manager/intent.py`) and starts the LangGraph desk with it (`request["ask"]` in `langgraph/desk.py`); Auto mode spends the funds round-robin (a share of each approved stock in turn), and "split evenly" in the user's words gives equal dollars (`sizing.size_purchases(..., allocation="even")`; `balance_evenly` is gone). In Ask mode (the default) the user is shown the final proposal and approves each trade at the terminal (entry 79: size first, ask last, no cash reserved; `--yes` never approves a trade). All agents are real; the account book is the real local Postgres.
- A request that NAMES THE TRADE ("Buy 50 shares of MSFT", "sell 10 shares of Tesla") is a DIRECT ORDER (entries 87 and 88), and so is a stock named with NO number ("I want to buy MSFT", "Buy $5,000 of MSFT"; entry 98: a PROGRAM decides in the user's words, `intent.RECOMMEND_CUE` and `as_direct_order`, not the model; a word that asks for a recommendation makes it a request to recommend; with no number Ask mode asks for it, `AccountManager._size_direct`, Auto spends the user's words, and a sale with no number is not placed): `understand_request` reads it as `action: "order"` with `orders` (a `symbol` or a `company`; code checks every field against the user's words: the shares as a number of shares right before the stock, the side from the last buying or selling word; a dollar amount is not shares), the desk goes `account_manager -> account_manager_direct -> trader` and skips the Data Clerk's NODE, the Advisor and the Critic (a test fails if any is built). The direct path skips the Data Clerk's node, so the Account Manager asks the Data Clerk (`DataClerk.request_symbols`, a `lookup_symbol` work order, exact matching, never a guess) for the company name and the symbol of each stock and APPENDS them to the work order (`AccountManager.name_the_stocks`: `data.companies` and the user's orders in `direct_orders`, each with both). `AccountManager.decide_direct` (`agents/account_manager/direct.py`) prices it and decides it `yes_user` with `origin: "direct"` (the user's own order is the approval); a COMPANY the user named is confirmed with them in Ask mode (symbol, company, shares, amount) and used without asking in Auto mode; a typed ticker is never asked about. `work_order.verify_trade` accepts such a decision only while `order.direct_orders` holds the user's matching record. NO PROFILE LIMIT EVER APPLIES to a direct order (Jacob; a test reads the source of the direct path), the cash and the holdings are the only limits, and it is placed as typed or not at all (the decision, `tickets.py` for a sale and `approve_trade` refuse; none cuts it). It is a market order with no market-hours check: TODO limit orders (Jacob). Also TODO (Jacob, 2026-11-15): stop-loss orders in the Trader and a monitoring mode in a future version (`design/week6_updated_design.md`); nothing is built. Real captures: `tests/fixtures/session_real/direct_order_forms.json` (the model's replies), `tests/fixtures/finnhub_company_search.json` (the symbol directory) and `bill_gates_direct_order*.json` (real sessions); tests `tests/test_direct_order.py`.

- `prometheus/tools/data_clerk.py` wraps the toolbox as tools; `prometheus/prompts/` holds the prompts; `prometheus/common/work_order.py` is the work order.
- A news article or press release that FAILS (a broken record, a bad time, a cut-off record) is discarded completely (entry 83): `rag/loaders.py` `load_news(path, skipped)` and `load_press_releases(path, skipped)` skip it and list it as a `Skipped`, the ingest prints what it discarded, and the toolbox discards a malformed API item or a corrupt market-news line (`failed` in its stats); filings keep the strict rule. ## Rules for changes

- Keep a rename or a move free of behaviour changes. Update every import, test and doc in the same change: `docs/design_choices.md`, `HowToRun.txt`, `README.md`, `design/week3_e2e.md`, `labs/module3_rag/how_to_do_langchain_rag.md`.
- A design decision gets a new numbered entry at the end of `docs/design_choices.md` (problem, decision, alternatives, consequences). Do not rewrite an old entry to look as if it had always said the new thing.
- Tests use REAL data: the real stored bars, the real captures in `tests/fixtures/` (made with `--save`), and for the agents the live tests (opt-in). Do not invent stock data (prices, metrics, news, companies) and do not add stand-in agents or models to a test. The older tests still have them; replacing those is a separate task.
- Keep an LLM out of anything a program can check: scores, citations and budgets are calculated in code (see entries 24 and 26).
- Leave no `__pycache__` or `.pytest_cache` behind. Keep `.md` and `.pdf` files.
